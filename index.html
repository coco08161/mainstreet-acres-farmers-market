Rooted in the Redland.

Mainstreet Acres Farmers Market brings local businesses, farmers, artisans, and entrepreneurs together in a fun, family‑friendly atmosphere.

We're located in the heart of Miami's Redland agricultural community. That means shoppers who come out on purpose: to buy local, try something new, and make a morning of it. For vendors, it's a place to meet those people face to face, introduce your products, and grow.

  • WhoFarmers, cottage bakers, specialty food makers, artisans, and local businesses.
  • WhenEvery Saturday & Sunday, 10 AM – 3 PM.
  • Where22700 SW 177th Avenue, Miami, FL 33170.

Who we welcome

Seven kinds of stalls. One good market.

We're looking for a healthy mix, so shoppers find something new at every table.

No. 01

Farmers & Growers

Fresh fruits, vegetables, herbs, plants, and flowers.

No. 02

Cottage Bakeries

Artisan breads, sourdough, cookies, cakes, pastries, and homemade baked goods.

No. 03

Specialty Foods

Honey, jams, sauces, dips, spreads, spices, and other locally made products.

No. 04

Dairy & Farm

Cheese, milk, eggs, and other locally sourced farm products.

No. 05

Arts & Crafts

Handmade jewelry, candles, pottery, home décor, artwork, and unique gifts.

No. 06

Health & Wellness

Natural skincare, handmade soaps, and wellness products.

No. 07

Local Businesses

Unique products and specialty vendors that complement the market experience.
