Requirements

What we look for.

dairy

Selling food, eggs, or dairy? Have your Florida license or permit details ready when you apply. Cottage food makers should label products as Florida law requires.

  • Quality and care.Products you're proud of, made, grown, or curated with real attention.

FAQ

Questions vendors ask.

Something else on your mind? Email stephanie@cmgproducts.net or call 305.222.2221.

When and where is the market?

Every Saturday and Sunday, 10 AM to 3 PM, at Mainstreet Acres, 22700 SW 177th Avenue, Miami, FL 33170, in the Redland.

Can I sell just one day, or only some weekends?

Tell us your preferred days and how often you'd like to come on the application. We'll work with you based on availability.

I'm just starting out. Can I still apply?

Yes. Whether you're an established business or just getting started, we'd love to hear from you.

Do I need a license to sell food?

All food and dairy vendors must comply with applicable Florida regulations and licensing requirements. If you're not sure what applies to you, mention it on your application and we can talk it through.

What about booth fees and setup?

Booth details, fees, and setup information are shared with vendors after their application is reviewed.

How will I hear back?

We review every application and reach out by email or phone. Questions in the meantime? Email stephanie@cmgproducts.net or call 305.222.2221.
